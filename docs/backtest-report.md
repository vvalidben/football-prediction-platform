> Premier League seasons from football-data.co.uk, loaded into a throwaway database
> through the normal ingestion path (no API-Football key is configured); predictions
> for 2021/22 to 2025/26. Command: `python -m app.ml backtest --start 2021-07-01
> --model elo --model poisson:alpha=3 --model logistic_regression:c=0.003,goal_alpha=10
> --model lightgbm:num_leaves=3,n_estimators=250`. Hyperparameters are the Sprint 6
> choices, tuned on data through 2024/25: only 2025/26 is fully free of look-ahead.
> Interpretation and the rolling-window comparison: [backtesting.md](backtesting.md).

## Protocol

- Period: 2021-07-01 to the last match; 179 retraining steps, one every 7 days.
- Training window: expanding (all earlier matches). At each step time t, models are fitted only on matches whose results were known at t (kickoff + 3 h <= t), and predict the matches kicking off before the next step, from feature snapshots taken at kickoff.
- Warm-up: training rows need both teams to have >= 3 rated matches; steps with fewer than 300 training matches predict nothing (0 matches skipped).
- Calibration (platt, isotonic): fitted at each step on the model's own earlier backtest predictions whose results were known at t; used once at least 300 exist, otherwise the raw probabilities are kept.
- Models and fixed hyperparameters: `elo`; `poisson(alpha=3)`; `logistic_regression(c=0.003, goal_alpha=10)`; `lightgbm(num_leaves=3, n_estimators=250)`. Hyperparameters chosen on data overlapping the backtest period are a look-ahead this framework cannot detect.
